Unnao rape case: Protest outside Delhi HC
against suspension of Sengar's jail term

Women activists from the All India Democratic Women's Association participated in the protest along with activist Yogita Bhayana and the survivor’s mother

Social activists stage a protest in front of High Court related to Unnao Case in New Delhi on December 26, 2025. | Photo Credit: Shashi Shekhar Kashyap

Scores of protesters, including women activists along with the Unnao rape survivor's mother,
demonstrated outside the Delhi High Court on Friday (December 26, 2025), days after the sentence of Kuldeep Sengar,
a former BJP MLA who was convicted in the case, was suspended.
